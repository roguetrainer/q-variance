### Key Features of the Q-Variance Literature

The q-variance formula states that the average variance over periods T with a price change x is given by σ² + z²/2 where z=x/√T. This relationship was:

1. **First predicted theoretically** using the quantum oscillator model before empirical testing
2. **Empirically validated** across multiple time scales (days to years)
3. **Applied to option pricing** to explain the volatility smile
4. **Extended to jump models** where all price movement occurs through quantum transitions

### Research Progression

The work progresses from:
- Initial quantum walk models (2020)
- Development of the quantum oscillator framework (2022)
- Discovery and validation of q-variance (2022-2024)
- Application to option pricing and implied volatility (2024-2025)
- Recent developments in quantum jump models (2025)

### Collaborative Work

Orrell has collaborated with Paul Wilmott, particularly on the paper "Q-Variance: or, a Duet Concerning the Two Chief World Systems" (2025), which presents a dialogue-style discussion of the q-variance phenomenon.
